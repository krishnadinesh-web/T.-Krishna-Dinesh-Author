About the author

Old stories, told again, in a voice meant for everyone who wants to sit down and listen.

T. Krishna Dinesh is an Indian author and management graduate whose books move between mythology, horror, romance, and quiet reflection. Working from the Puranas and India's great epics, he retells old stories in plain, welcoming English — for readers meeting them for the first time and for those returning to them once more.
